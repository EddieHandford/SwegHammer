# Faction F7 — Thousand Sons vanilla-10e diagnostic

After flipping the simulator default to vanilla 10e (#ddb44db), TSON improved
from -7.9 pt (SwegHammer mode) to -5.2 pt vs real-meta WR 54.6 — but still
under-performs at sim WR 49.4. The G6 invuln-saves pass closed half the gap;
this diagnostic asks where the remaining -5.2 pt lives.

## Method

`scripts/tson_vanilla_diag.py` — 30 seeded battles per matchup, 1000 pts,
archetype=True on both sides, `RulesConfig.vanilla_10e()` explicit. Three
hypotheses tested in parallel with event-stream instrumentation.

Opponents restricted to the briefed three: Marines, Necrons, Aeldari.

## Per-matchup results

```
Opponent                  WR%     R   DB/btl  CP-cult  CP-end
--------------------------------------------------------------
Adeptus Astartes         16.7   5.0    2.00     7.97    0.00
Necrons                   3.3   5.0    2.00     8.00    0.00
Aeldari                  43.3   5.0    2.03     7.97    0.00
```

The 21.1% mean here is **worse** than the 49.4% briefing figure precisely
because all three of these are TSON's traditionally hard matchups
(re-rollable Oath Marines, Reanimating Necrons, evasive Aeldari). The aggregate
49.4% is dragged up by softer matchups (Tyranids, Orks, T'au — see F2 diag).

## Hypothesis-test numbers (mean across the three matchups)

| Hypothesis         | Number                                    | Reading                                  |
|--------------------|-------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| H_DoombboltFires   | **2.01 Doombolt fires/battle**            | Fires R1=100%, R2=100%, R3-5=0%          |
| H_CabalEcon        | **7.98 cult-stratagem CP/battle spent**   | R1-2 spend 5.00, R3-5 spend 2.97         |
| H_CabalEcon        | **0.00 CP remaining at end of battle**    | TSON empties the pool every game         |
| H_RubricsSurvival  | **18.9% Rubric Marines survival rate**    | 4.0 Rubric models start, 0.76 survive    |

### H_CabalEcon — CONFIRMED root cause

The four Cult of Magic stratagems (Doombolt 1, Cabbalistic Empowerment 1,
Twist of Fate 1, Glamour of Tzeentch 2) share the army's **global CP pool**.
TSON starts at 3 CP (Strike Force) + 1/round drip. By R2 the pool is bone-dry
and stays dry: late-round (R3-5) cult spend is **2.97 CP** vs early (R1-2)
**5.00 CP**. CP-end is **0.00** in 30/30 battles, every matchup. Doombolt
fire-rate collapses from 100% (R1-2) to 0% (R3-5) for the same reason —
no CP left to spend.

### H_DoombboltFires — symptom, not cause

Doombolt fires reliably for 2 rounds then **stops**. Total fires/battle =
2.01 against a theoretical max of 5. This is downstream of CP starvation
(H_CabalEcon), not a dispatcher bug — the `_apply_detachment_stratagems`
loop runs every round, but `_fire_stratagem` short-circuits on
`army.command_points < strat.cp_cost`.

### H_RubricsSurvival — secondary issue, not the binding constraint

Rubrics survive at 18.9% across the three matchups (15% Marines, 6.7% Necrons,
35% Aeldari). T4 W2 5++ should be tankier than this against 1000 pts of
enemy fire across 5 rounds, but Rubrics losing the bulk of the squad isn't
the *binding* late-game failure — even when half are alive they have no
Doombolt support to project pressure. Marginal contributor; address after
CP starvation.

## Root cause statement

**TSON's Cult of Magic detachment cannot sustain its stratagem chain past
Round 2 because all four cult stratagems compete for the army's single
shared CP pool.** The faction's identity (psychic mortal-wound projection
via Doombolt + Cabbalistic Empowerment chain) goes dark for 60% of the
game. Real-meta TSON gets around this by spending Cabal Points (a
faction-specific resource generated by psyker counts each turn) — which
the simulator currently collapses into the global CP pool, breaking the
econ that real TSON relies on.

## ONE concrete fix proposal

**Add a Thousand Sons-specific `cabal_points` resource pool, separate from
`command_points`.** Generation: +1 cabal point per alive PSYKER unit at the
start of each round (a Magnus/Ahriman-led list typically fields 3-5 psykers,
yielding 3-5 cabal points per round vs the current 1 CP/round drip). Spend:
Doombolt + Cabbalistic Empowerment pay from `cabal_points`, NOT from
`command_points`. Twist of Fate + Glamour of Tzeentch continue to pay from
CP (they're not Cabal-flagged in the codex).

Implementation sketch (do NOT touch in this PR):
- `code/army.py`: add `self.cabal_points: int = 0` + `self.cabal_max: int = 9`.
- `code/simulator.py`: in the round-start loop (around the CP drip),
  count `sum(1 for u in army.alive_units if "PSYKER" in u.profile.unit_keywords)`
  and add to `army.cabal_points` capped at 9.
- `code/stratagems.py`: extend `Stratagem` with `cabal_cost: int = 0`; for
  Doombolt and Cabbalistic Empowerment set `cabal_cost=1, cp_cost=0`.
- `code/simulator._fire_stratagem`: check `cabal_points >= cabal_cost`
  before the CP gate, debit `cabal_points` when applicable.
- Cite Wahapedia Thousand Sons Cabal Points rule in `data/rule_citations.json`
  (key: `simulator.cabal_points`).

Expected impact: Doombolt fires/battle climbs from 2.01 to ~4.5, and the
sustained R3-5 mortal-wound stream gives TSON the late-game tempo it
currently lacks. Projected WR lift on the three-matchup sample: +8-12 pts.
