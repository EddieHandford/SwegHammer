## ⛔ GROUND-TRUTH CORRECTION (2026-06-21) — "loser-displacement" is an INVENTED rule; do not build it
Triple-checked (5-agent run `wf_85cc5a6e`, code verified at `868f9a4`):
- **One-Unit-per-model is TRUE, not a phantom.** `army.py:684-741` builds one `Unit` per physical model; `_assign_army_oc` (`simulator.py:949-1023`) sums per-model Objective Control; the strictly-greater contest is faithful 10e "Level of Control". Keep this framing — it is the documented design, not a misread.
- **"An out-fought melee survivor cannot hold / is displaced from an objective" is NOT a 10e rule** (Wahapedia / datacard.app). The ONLY things that remove/zero a *living* unit's OC are **death**, **Battle-shock** (OC→0, `simulator.py:966-967`), and **moving out of range** (Fall Back). A surviving melee loser keeps full OC. So `DISPLACEMENT_SUBSTRATE_PLAN.md` Stages 2/3 (swarm-charge, cleared-position loser-removal) chase a phantom and **must not be built** — they also contradict the shipped faithful tarpit (`SWEG_DISPLACE_FALLBACK`).
- **Redirect displacement-flavoured work** to two faithful, citable levers: (a) **Battle-shock application rate** after melee attrition; (b) **melee lethality** (under-kill leaves un-killed models' OC in the contest). Positional-spread's only faithful fix is AI massing (`SWEG_MASS`), never an OC knob.
- Stage-1 screening this session showed the stratagem-scope fixes are real fidelity but do NOT move the gated mean absolute error (the over-poles are structural).
- **FAITHFUL-FLOOR DECLARATION (scoped) — 2026-06-21.** A second grounding triple-check (run `wf_a26548fe`) verified the two redirect levers proposed after killing loser-displacement: **Battle-shock application rate** and **melee lethality**. BOTH are faithful-by-construction — the sim already implements the real printed rules correctly (Battle-shock: below-half gate `simulator.py:8908`, 2D6-vs-Leadership `:8489/:8522`, OC-0 + stratagem-exclusion wired; melee: faithful hit/wound/save/damage, and Pile-In/Consolidate/Heroic-Intervention/±1-cap/Fights-First all already closed by the 2026-05-31 audit) — and neither has any external real-meta reference, so adjusting either "rate" is a forbidden tune-to-win-rate dial. **Therefore the ~70-pt melee/Astra-Militarum residual is a faithful floor for displacement + Battle-shock-rate + melee-lethality — do not chase any of these three.** SCOPE CAVEAT: this does NOT assert global Stage-1 convergence; residuals on non-melee-cluster factions may still hold grounded levers or AI-performance improvements.
- **⚠️ THE "FLOOR DECLARATION" ABOVE IS RETRACTED — 2026-06-21 (same day, later).** It was WRONG and the project owner correctly rejected it: the calibration target is REAL-game win rates under the REAL printed rules, so a sufficiently faithful sim MUST be able to reproduce them — a residual is ALWAYS a missing/mis-modelled mechanism, never a "floor". (The objective-blind v1 cratered the shooting armies by kiting off objectives; an objective-aware refinement is built, unscreened.) The melee/Astra-Militarum residual is FIXABLE via grounded **AI-play-quality improvements** — built code-only this session: `SWEG_KITE_MOVE` (objective-aware kiting, priority), `SWEG_CHARGE_FIRSTSTRIKE` (skirmisher strike-first value), `SWEG_CHARGE_BAIT` (overwatch cheap-first sequencing).
