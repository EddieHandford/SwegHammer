## 2. Synthesis: what real competitive lists look like

### Dominant detachment(s)

| Detachment | Evidence | Share estimate |
|---|---|---|
| **Warhost** | Multiple tournament wins (Salt City, Nova Open, Michigan Grand Tournament) through most of 2025. Multiple top-5 placements. Goonhammer description: "quickly becoming the most popular." | ~40-50% of competitive Aeldari through August 2025. Still present late 2025. |
| **Aspect Host** | Rose after Q4 2025 dataslate; described as the dominant shift post-December 2025. Won Hammer Cup. | ~35-45% of competitive Aeldari after December 2025. |
| **Devoted of Ynnead** | Niche Ynnari-specific detachment; reported 65% win rate (Stat Check, Warp Friends) but very low representation. Tournament wins cited but small sample. | ~5-15% representation. |

**Conclusion:** Warhost and Aspect Host split the competitive field roughly equally across the May 2026 window, with Warhost slightly dominant in the first half of 2025. The Warp Friends aggregate (41.5% win rate) covers both — both detachments converge to the same canonical unit spine.

### Canonical unit core

Across all 6 confirmed tournament lists (Sources 1-5, 8), the following units appear consistently:

| Unit | Appearances | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fuegan | 5/6 | Near-universal; leads Fire Dragons |
| Jain Zar | 5/6 | Near-universal; leads Howling Banshees |
| Lhykhis | 4/6 | Very common; leads Warp Spiders |
| Warp Spiders | 6/6 | Universal (×2-3 squads) |
| Fire Dragons | 6/6 | Universal (×2 squads) |
| Dark Reapers | 5/6 | Near-universal (×1-2 squads) |
| Swooping Hawks | 4/6 | Common (×2-3 squads) |
| Howling Banshees | 5/6 | Near-universal (×1-2 squads) |
| Wave Serpent | 6/6 | Universal (×1, sometimes 2) |
| Corsair Voidreavers | 4/6 | Common battleline filler (×1-2 squads) |
| Rangers | 5/6 | Near-universal (×1-2 squads) |
| Autarch / Autarch Wayleaper | 3/6 | Common character support |
| Eldrad Ulthran | 3/6 | Appears in the "five Phoenix Lord" variants |
| Maugan Ra | 2/6 | Less common; present in multi-Phoenix-Lord builds |
| Fire Prism | 2/6 | Artillery support (Aspect Host builds) |

### The Yncarne in competitive lists

- **Warhost lists: zero appearances** across all confirmed tournament lists (Sources 1, 3, 4).
- **Aspect Host lists: zero appearances** (Sources 2, 7, 8).
- **Devoted of Ynnead lists: present as WARLORD** (Source 5) — this is the Ynnari-specific detachment.

The Yncarne is exclusively a Devoted of Ynnead unit in competitive play. It does not appear in any confirmed Warhost or Aspect Host list. Its inclusion at count=4 (highest priority) in the current "Battle Host" template is the primary list-realism error.

### The Avatar of Khaine in competitive lists

- **Zero appearances** across all 6 confirmed tournament lists from any detachment. Source 2 (Grimhammer Tactics faction guide) explicitly says "the Avatar is better suited to be run in a Warhost build" but does NOT include it in the recommended Warhost list either. Its inclusion at count=3 in the current template is not supported by the evidence.

The Aspect Host detachment is an equally valid rename, but Warhost has slightly more representation in the early-to-mid 2025 window the Warp Friends target covers, and the canonical template structure maps to either.
