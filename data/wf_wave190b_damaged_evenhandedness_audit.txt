WAVE 190b AUDIT (task #74) — even-handedness of the wave-188 SWEG_DMGHIT (Knight damaged -1-to-Hit)

QUESTION: do OTHER factions big models have the same degrading-hit-roll Damaged bracket?

METHOD: decompress BSData v10.6.0 .cat.gz, count datasheets whose Damaged: X-Y wounds bracket contains the
OFFENSIVE self-penalty "this model makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll" (de-conflated from the
DEFENSIVE "attack TARGETS that unit" -1-to-be-hit, which is a different rule).

VERBATIM non-Knight example (T au Stormsurge, MONSTER) — IDENTICAL combined bracket the sim models Knight-only:
  "While this model has 1-5 wounds remaining, subtract 3 from this models Objective Control characteristic,
   and each time this model makes an attack, subtract 1 from the Hit roll."
  (= both the OC half [wave-85 SWEG_DMGOC] AND the Hit half [wave-188 SWEG_DMGHIT], both currently Knight-gated)

CAVEAT: 94 is a heuristic lower bound (one phrase form, 400-char window); includes ~3 Astartes-Heresy-Legends
(not fielded, exclude). Qualitative conclusion robust: ~88 NON-Knight datasheets share the rule.

IMPLICATION: wave-188 SWEG_DMGHIT (default-ON, Knight-gated) is a faithful-but-PARTIAL implementation of a
universal rule. Running it Knight-only biases the sim toward degrading ONLY Knight offense, not their durable
opponents (Custodes dreadnoughts 6, T au Stormsurge/Riptide 6, World Eaters 3, AdMech 3, etc). FAITHFUL FIX =
generalize the damaged bracket (OC + Hit) to every model with a real bracket — needs a mapper extraction of
per-unit damaged thresholds + degraded stats. Even-handed (touches BOTH poles): nerfs over-shooters big models
(Custodes/T au/World Eaters) AND under-shooters durable vehicles (AdMech/Necrons). Net effect must be measured.
